Many Lights

un film en cinq lumières

1 min 52 s · painted & scored live

00:00:00

    A love story in five lights. A rower at dawn and the woman in rose who waves back; a meeting on the garden bridge; an umbrella in the rain; a waltz under the lanterns; and, many years later, the same lantern beside them on a bench as the fireflies rise. Their melody returns in every chapter: piano at sunrise, violin in the garden, cello in the rain, the accordion at the dance, and the cello again under the stars.

    Each frame is painted from about a thousand fresh brush strokes laid over the last, sampled from a small sketch of the scene the way a painter squints at a motif. The sound is synthesized from the same score as the picture: every glint on the water, every lamp, lantern beat and firefly is heard where it is seen. Headphones help.
